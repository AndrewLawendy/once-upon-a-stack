What we don't know yet

Things this book couldn't settle from primary sources, and what would.

A public stampede post-mortem. No dated, public incident report of a cache-expiry stampede was found. Facebook's 23 Sep 2010 outage was a refill feedback loop, not an expiry. Settle: a post-mortem collection such as danluu/post-mortems. p.

A stale-cache post-mortem. Candidates (GitLab, Jan 2022) were seen only as search snippets. Settle: read GitLab production issue 6182. p.

What happens to a lost lease. The memcache paper doesn't say what happens if the token holder never sets the value. Settle: memcached lease code at Facebook (not public) or the authors. p.

How long "wait a short time" is. The paper doesn't give the retry delay for clients told to wait. p.

How long stale values are kept. Facebook keeps recently deleted values "for a short time", with no number. p.

Facebook's overall hit rate. Not found in the parts of the paper read. Settle: its section 7, workload. p.
